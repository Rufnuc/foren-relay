FOREN WEEKLY USAGE

Public verification of aggregate, anonymous product-usage reports and their blockchain commitments.

This report contains aggregate usage statistics generated from anonymous cryptographic identities. An anonymous identity does not necessarily represent a unique human — one person may create several. The blockchain commitment verifies the integrity and existence of this report at a point in time; it does not independently verify that every underlying action was performed by a different person, that any action was performed by a human at all, or that any incident record is truthful, legally admissible, or otherwise validated.
