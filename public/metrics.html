These figures are computed from anonymous cryptographic identities. An anonymous identity does not necessarily represent a unique human — one person may create several, and referral counts reflect link redemptions, not verified individuals. Figures are aggregate only and contain no personal, location, or incident data. The K-factor shown is a proxy (distinct redeemers ÷ active identities), not a cohort-tracked viral coefficient.
